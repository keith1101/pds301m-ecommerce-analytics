## 3. Intentional differences

- **Guest £1,754,901.91** (~16.5% of total): Guest rows count toward revenue but are excluded from RFM per the data contract. This is the largest difference and is fully intentional.
- **Service codes** (DOT, POST, M, m, AMAZONFEE, B): kept in total revenue, excluded from the product ranking. 90.3% of service-code rows are Guest, so they barely affect RFM; the remainder (~£38K, < 0.5% of total Monetary) is negligible.
- **Cancelled invoices and invalid rows**: outside the computed population at every step; counts match the pipeline audit report.
